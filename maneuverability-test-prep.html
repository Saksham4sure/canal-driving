Crack The Cones

Master The Most Failed Portion of the Ohio Driving Exam

The State of Ohio is one of the few states that requires student drivers to pass a specialized 5-marker cone course called the Maneuverability Test in addition to the road test. Because high school parking lots and home driveways rarely have the exact 9x20-foot dimensions or weighted markers, students often guess their steering angles and fail.

At Canal Driving School, we break down maneuverability into pure, simple geometry. We teach you clear visual anchor points—aligning your side mirrors with the nose cone, using your rear window edges, and applying exact steering turns—so that passing becomes completely predictable.

The 3 Stages of the Ohio Maneuverability Course

1

Stage 1: Pulling Forward & Examiner Command

You drive straight forward through the 9x20-foot box between the four corner markers. As you approach the center point cone, the BMV examiner will instruct you to steer either to the "Right" or "Left".

2

Stage 2: Parallel Alignment & Stop

You steer to the designated side, straighten the vehicle completely parallel to the course lines, and stop with your rear bumper even with the point marker cone.

3

Stage 3: Reverse Backing & Clear Exit

From the stopped parallel position, you shift into reverse, back straight past the point cone, counter-steer back into the center box, straighten your wheels, and back straight out without bumping or straddling any markers.

Understanding Ohio BMV Scoring Penalties

Passing the Ohio test requires having 25 or fewer points deducted. Here is what examiners look for during maneuverability:

  • ✗ Knocking over or running over a cone: Immediate test failure.
  • ! Stopping unnecessarily: Deductions for hesitation or stopping when not required.
  • ! Vehicle not parallel: Points lost if vehicle is angled relative to the course markers.
  • ! Failure to clear the nose cone: Bumping into the center marker when backing.

Maneuverability FAQs

How long is a maneuverability prep session?

Sessions are typically 1 to 2 hours long. In a single 2-hour session, students generally run through the cone course 15 to 25 times and complete a full mock road exam.

Is this open to both teens and adults?

Yes! Whether you are a 16-year-old teen taking your test for the first time, or an adult learner preparing for your BMV exam, our maneuverability prep is customized for any age.
